Growth is only about 11% while the stock trades near 10x EV/revenue and over 100x GAAP earnings; FCF yield is about 3% and about 1.5% after SBC, so valuation leaves little room for a growth miss. Net retention is 107%, so growth leans on new logos in a contested identity market. FCF benefits from collections timing (accounts receivable fell 687M to 469M seasonally; deferred revenue fell 124M in H1). Goodwill is 5,487M of 9,138M assets (Auth0). Buybacks of 372M roughly offset dilution rather than shrinking share count much (Class A 167.7M in April to 167.1M in July). The stock fell 4.3% on 11-16 and was down again intraday 11-17 (170.50 at 12:05 ET, context only); the cause was not found in filings, and no 8-K exists since 2026-09-18.

## Invalidation
