## Countercase
Earnings quality is rental growth in one segment (TRS) that depends on data center and electronics demand, while the two largest legacy businesses are flat to weak; revenue guidance was cut. Capex is being raised by about 70M while FCF is negative and debt rose 103M in nine months, with only 1M cash; the new notes cost 6.53%, above the equity yield. Net income growth of 2.8% in nine months does not justify 20x. Management transition (CEO) adds uncertainty. Used equipment sale gains, depreciation assumptions and fleet quality were not examined, so earnings may be overstated if equipment lives are long. Peers, MD&A, debt note covenant ratios and 10-K were not read.

## Invalidation
