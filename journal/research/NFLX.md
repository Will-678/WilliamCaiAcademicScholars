## Countercase
Q3 operating margin was helped by expense timing, so 34.6% is not a run rate; Q4 guide is 26%. Ad revenue target was cut about 10%, which weakens a key growth leg. FCF guide was cut by 0.5B. TTM GAAP earnings include a 2.8B one-off fee, so 17x flatters. Content amortization is a large accounting judgment (34.6B content assets); cash content spend ratio 1.1x means cash outlays exceed expense. Buybacks (10.8B in 9M26, 4.8B in Q3) are financed partly by rising debt (gross 15.3B, up 1B in Q3) and exceed FCF in Q3. Competition (YouTube, Disney, Amazon, Paramount-WBD combination) and price increase fatigue not examined; the letter does not report subscribers. Peers and Netflix's own multiple history not compared. Legal notes, MD&A, 10-K risk factors and content commitments were not read. 1-day sample of quote context only; price is near the low of the 8 sessions viewed but no 52-week data was obtained.

## Invalidation
