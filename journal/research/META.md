## Countercase
Capex has doubled and now consumes about 85% of OCF; SBC of 26.9B exceeds TTM FCF, so owner earnings after SBC are about zero. The company is now raising external capital (equity forward, preferred, euro and dollar bonds), which reverses the 2025 buyback policy and signals that internal cash no longer funds the AI build. Operating income fell 37% in Q3 even after backing out the one-off legal charge (cost growth outpaces revenue), legal exposure continues (New Mexico verdict, personal-injury bellwethers March and August 2027, remaining state cases), and the Q3 2025 tax charge flatters y/y net income comparison. Property and equipment depreciation will rise sharply. The Class C recapitalization and founder alignment agreement concentrate control. Segment data (Reality Labs losses), depreciation schedule, peers (GOOGL, AMZN, MSFT, PINS, SNAP) and guidance detail are unread.

## What would change the view
Thesis of a quality compounder at a reasonable multiple breaks if revenue growth falls toward low double digits while capex stays above 135B, if financing needs grow, or if legal costs recur. A price case needs evidence that AI capex earns returns (revenue per ad and impressions growth sustained) and visible FCF recovery in 2027.
