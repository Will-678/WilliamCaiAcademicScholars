## Counterthesis
Growth leans on asset based fees (27% of run rate, +22%) that fall with equity markets. Sustainability and Climate retention is slipping (92.2% vs 94.0%). Net debt about 6.5B with FCF fully devoted to buybacks and dividends, and the revolver drawn 810M vs 300M at year end, so capital return is partly debt financed (1,583M of buybacks and dividends in 9M26 vs 9M FCF of about 990M after software). Negative equity makes book multiples meaningless. Deferred revenue fell to 1,036.7M from 1,231.8M (seasonal). Capitalized software rose to 96.7M from 66.7M, which flatters operating cash flow relative to expense. Competition from index and data peers was not compared.

## Invalidation
Invalidated if retention falls below about 92% in Index or Analytics, asset based fees decline sharply with markets, interest costs on rising debt compress FCF, or buybacks continue to outrun FCF so that leverage rises.
