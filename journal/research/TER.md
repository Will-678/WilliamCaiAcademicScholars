## Counterthesis
TTM earnings are upswing earnings: 9M26 net income is 3.8x 9M25, and Q4 guidance midpoint implies EPS below Q3, so the growth rate is peaking in sequential terms. Semiconductor test demand is lumpy and tied to a few AI customers' capex; a pause would hit revenue and the 59% gross margin quickly. Inventory rose 51% (575.0M from 379.6M) and goodwill 27% (663.8M from 521.0M, 165.6M of 9M acquisitions) while redeemable noncontrolling interests of 49.1M appeared, so working capital and acquisition accounting need review. Buybacks fell to 173.5M for 9M26 from 518.7M, so cash is not being returned at the pace of last year. FCF yield of 2.8% is low. Customer concentration, export controls, the 11% price drop in six sessions, and Forms 144 and 4 seen in the filing list (11-16, 11-17) are unread.

## Invalidation
The thesis is invalidated if Q1 2027 revenue guidance falls well below the Q4 range of 1,250M to 1,350M, if gross margin falls below about 55%, if inventory keeps growing faster than revenue, if AI test customers cut capex, or if TTM earnings normalize so that the multiple on mid-cycle earnings exceeds about 40x.
