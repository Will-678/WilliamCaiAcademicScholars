## Countercase
Substantially all revenue is .com and .net under the Cooperative Agreement with the US Department of Commerce and the ICANN .com Registry Agreement; the 10-Q states pricing provisions have been challenged and could face challenges again, up to 7% annual increases are permitted only in the final four years of each six year period and are not assured, and loss or modification of the rights would be material. Growth is price plus domain base; 9M OCF fell 3.6% (772.3M vs 801.5M) on higher payroll, vendor and cash taxes including Switzerland Pillar 2. Q3 NI flat. AI and alternative online identities could weaken registration demand. 32x earnings and 3.7% FCF yield leave little margin of safety against a mid single digit grower. Leverage is moderate on cash flow but equity is negative, so buybacks are funded partly by debt. Peers and valuation history not compared.

## Invalidation
Invalidated if domain base growth turns negative or renewal rates fall, if Commerce or ICANN restrict .com pricing, if operating margin drops below about 65%, or if buybacks require debt beyond cash flow.
