## Countercase
GAAP margins fell sharply after the acquisitions (Q3 operating margin 9.4% vs 16.8%), SBC is about 15% of revenue, so FCF after SBC yield is only 2.3%. Reported 2026 growth is inflated by 7.6B Armis and 1.2B Veza; organic growth is unread. 9M net income includes 367M of upward strategic-investment gains. Goodwill rose from 3.6B to 10.0B and intangibles from 1.1B to 3.6B, so impairment risk and amortization drag now matter. One US federal channel partner is 12% of revenue and 20% of receivables. A workforce restructuring started in 2026 (amount unread). Concern about AI agents displacing seat-based software may explain the de-rating and has not been tested. Peers, guidance, cRPO, risk factors and the 10-K unread.

## What would change the view
Thesis weakens if organic subscription growth falls below high teens, GAAP operating margin stays below 12% after integration, federal concentration cuts hit revenue, or buybacks keep being funded by commercial paper. A valuation case needs FCF yield after SBC well above 4% or evidence of stable margin recovery.
