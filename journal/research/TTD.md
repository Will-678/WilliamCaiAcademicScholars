## Countercase
Revenue is now flat to down (Q3 -0.5%, Q4 guide about -11%) and the 4% 9M growth includes 15M non-recurring partner revenue and a shift of costs from revenue reductions into platform operations. Operating income fell 21.5% in 9M and Adjusted EBITDA margin dropped from 39% to 33%, so TTM FCF and earnings overstate the run rate. FCF after SBC is only about 5.8% of market cap because SBC is 14% of TTM revenue. Net income is helped by a longer equipment life (about 7M) and an 18M asset sale gain, and operating cash flow by a 203M working capital inflow (receivables collected, payables paid). The 2026 restructuring cost 44M, 14.4M options were repriced on 2026-10-19 (incremental expense not yet measured), and a CEO performance option was struck at 14.97. Securities class actions over 2023 to 2025 statements and a Delaware reincorporation suit are pending. Competition from Amazon and walled gardens, and net versus gross revenue presentation changes, could keep growth below 10%. The 15% two day rebound leaves little margin for a failed recovery.

## What would invalidate it
Q4 revenue below the 755M floor, 2027 growth that stays negative or low single digit, Adjusted EBITDA margin staying under about 33%, retention falling below 95%, FCF after SBC turning negative, or an adverse ruling or large settlement in the securities class action. Conversely, revenue growth returning to double digits would change the thesis to a recovery case.
