## Countercase
GAAP profit is thin: 9M 2026 operating income was -0.2M on 476.2M revenue, TTM pretax income 32.3M, and reported net income depends on a large deferred tax asset (143.2M) and a 17.5M non-operating fair value gain in Q3. Stock compensation (59.2M in 9M, 12% of revenue) is nearly as large as FCF, so FCF after SBC is under 1% of market cap. Advertising growth is largely acquired (Nativo) and its guidance range was narrowed down (98 to 102M from 98 to 115M); hardware units fell 32% after exiting brick-and-mortar retail; advertising gross margin is low (Q3 cost 11.5M on 25.4M revenue). Q3 OCF 27.2M was below Adjusted EBITDA 34.6M. Related party and Aura arrangements are growing (related party revenue, deferred revenue and fair value gains). Convertible notes are 0% with a conversion price near 81 against a 42 stock, so they are debt not equity for now; maturity 2030. Competition from Apple and Google location features and Tile-related privacy litigation (arbitration compelled, no accrual). Share count rose 2.8% from year-end despite 44.4M of buybacks. Revenue growth of 36 to 39% must continue to justify about 24x Adjusted EBITDA.
