## Countercase
Revenue growth has slowed to 4-5% (recurring +4%, customer count -15% largely from the divestiture); a mid-single-digit grower with 62.9M TTM SBC (8% of sales) is not obviously cheap at 4.8% FCF yield after SBC and 27-35x GAAP earnings. Non-GAAP EPS of 5.08 excludes SBC and amortization, so the 16x multiple is flattered. Margin gains in 2026 partly reflect the divestiture and cost control; Q2 GAAP NI was only 6.9M. Retail customer concentration/tariff-driven volume risk, AI-driven disruption of EDI software, and large buybacks at higher prices than the 2025 low are risks. Peer multiples (descriptions: Descartes, Manhattan, Kinaxis, Appfolio, Paycom) and 10-K risk factors/audit matters were not read; CEO 10b5-1 plan and leadership transition are unassessed.

## Invalidation
Valuation case would need FCF yield after SBC above about 6% or evidence growth reaccelerates above 8% organically. Quality thesis fails if organic recurring revenue growth falls below 3%, adjusted EBITDA margin drops below 30%, or SBC rises faster than revenue.
