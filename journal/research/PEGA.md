## Countercase
The Appian trade secret case is a retrial set to start 2027-01-11. On 2026-10-16 the court let Appian present a reasonable royalty theory seeking 284.6M for marketing and sales activities and 1.36B for Pega Platform revisions (total about 1.64B, roughly 32% of market cap, versus 309.7M of cash and securities); the 2022 verdict was 2.06B before it was reversed on appeal. The 10-Q states management cannot estimate the range of loss and has accrued nothing for it. 9M operating income fell to 68.6M from 158.7M (G&A +21.5%, selling +9.3%, a 16.0M settlement charge); 9M OCF fell 12% and FCF 14% (289.8M vs 338.3M); the 10-Q warns cash flow may be adversely affected for the rest of the year. Total revenue is flat over nine months, license revenue is shrinking, 2026 buybacks of 432.7M exceeded 9M FCF and cut cash by 116M, and the release gave no forward guidance in the exhibit read. Stock is -9.4% from 2026-11-04 (34.36) to 2026-11-09 (31.13) for reasons not verified.

## Invalidation
A retrial verdict or settlement in the low billions, ACV growth falling below about 4%, Pega Cloud ACV growth below about 15%, a further decline in FCF or operating margin, or buybacks continuing to outrun cash. Conversely, a defence verdict would remove the main overhang. Retrial starts 2027-01-11; the defamation jury trial is scheduled for 2026-11-30 (per the 10-Q).
