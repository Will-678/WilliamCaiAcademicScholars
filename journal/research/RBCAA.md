## Thesis and evidence

Price: close 88.95 on 2026-11-17 (from 91.25 on 11-13). The price assumes steady core bank earnings; the possible mispricing is that the market may be discounting the high loss consumer credit line, but this has not been shown. Cash paid as dividends was 27.8M for 9M26 (about 26% of 9M earnings).

## Counterthesis
Core Bank nonperforming loans rose to 0.63% of loans from 0.42% a year earlier and the total allowance ratio rose to 1.73% from 1.51%, so credit is deteriorating from a low base. Republic Credit Solutions is a high loss unsecured product (allowance 17.8%) whose provision more than doubled and whose profit fell 15%; regulatory or consumer finance scrutiny of tax refund and line of credit products is a standing risk. 9M earnings fell 2% and ROE slid from 13.8% to 12.3%, so growth is flat. Class A and B dual share structure and family control limit minority influence. Share counts, deposit mix, uninsured deposits, securities unrealized losses, tax refund season earnings, and the 10-Q notes were not read; the 10-Q itself exceeded the fetch limit.

## Invalidation
The idea fails if Core Bank nonperforming loans keep rising above about 1% of loans, if RCS provisions keep growing faster than its revenue, if regulators restrict tax refund or line of credit products, if TTM earnings fall below about 120M, or if price rises to more than about 2x book without higher returns.
