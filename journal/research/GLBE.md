## Counterthesis
Reported growth includes an acquisition (Passport; organic split not read). 9M OCF was only 69.2M against 9M adjusted EBITDA 176.3M, so cash conversion in 2026 depends on a strong Q4 and on merchant fund timing; TTM FCF leans on Q4 2025. GAAP profit relies on a 50.9M tax benefit. Non-GAAP gross margin fell to 44.8% from 46.3% and G&A rose 85%. Revenue depends on Shopify Managed Markets and a few large merchants (concentration unread); fulfillment revenue (57% of total) is lower margin pass through. Adjusted EBITDA excludes SBC, acquisition amortization and contingent consideration. Global tariffs, de minimis changes and consumer demand can cut GMV.

## Invalidation
Invalidated if GMV growth falls below about 20%, Q4 revenue lands below the 464M guide low end, adjusted EBITDA margin falls under 18%, full year OCF fails to reach about 250M, or a major merchant or Shopify arrangement is lost.
