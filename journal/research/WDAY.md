## Counterthesis
Owner earnings are much lower than headline FCF: SBC is 16% of revenue and the buyback (2.9B in 6M) mostly offsets dilution; FCF after SBC yield is 2.7%. GAAP operating margin is only about 11%. Net income is flattered by a one-time deferred tax asset. New notes carry 5.7% to 6.5% coupons, about double the retiring 3.5% to 3.8% notes, so the company is raising debt to fund buybacks at a high cost; debt rises from 3.0B to about 5.5B before the 2027 retirement. AI agents may pressure seat-based HCM and finance software; growth near 13% with a restructuring and ConductorOne identity acquisition (price undisclosed) suggests defence of position. Cause of the 5.8% drop on 11-17 unknown.
