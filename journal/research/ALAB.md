## Countercase
At 25x sales and 87x earnings, any deceleration, a customer shifting to in-house or competitor silicon (Broadcom, Marvell, Credo), or lower AI capex would cut the multiple sharply. Customer A was 53% of Q3 revenue and 58% of receivables, and the 10-Q says concentration moves between manufacturing partners, so end-customer exposure is not fully visible. SBC is 195M in nine months (15% of revenue) with about 10M dilutive shares above basic; warrants issued to customers reduce revenue (30.4M) and signal pricing concessions. Working capital absorbed more than a third of net income plus SBC; inventory is 3.4x year-end. GAAP profit is helped by tax benefits and a valuation allowance that is still in place. ## Invalidation
Revenue growth below about 50% year on year, gross margin below 70%, a top customer falling below 30% share without replacement, inventory and receivables growing faster than revenue for two quarters, or export-control restrictions on China-linked customers. A price decline to a multiple that peers justify would change the valuation view but not the quality view.
