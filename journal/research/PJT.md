## Countercase
Advisory revenue is lumpy and cyclical: Q3 fell 14% and pretax income 24% year on year, so the TTM and 9M figures flatter the run rate. TTM OCF and FCF are inflated by accrued compensation timing (OCF 576.9M in 9M 2026 vs 370.6M in 9M 2025) and a bonus cycle, while stock compensation (241M TTM) is a real cost and unit exchanges plus buybacks consumed about 169.4M and 134.7M of cash in 9M 2026 to offset dilution. Up-C structure: non-controlling interests of 688.4M and a tax receivable agreement liability of 36.4M complicate per share earnings; GAAP diluted EPS treats partnership units as anti-dilutive in some periods (diluted shares 28.7M for 9M 2026 vs 40.7M fully exchanged), so reported EPS and a share count based on Class A only would overstate per share value. Non-compensation costs rose 10% in Q3 while revenue fell. Operating leases are 429M. Competitors (Lazard, Evercore, Moelis, Houlihan Lokey, Goldman, boutiques) compete for talent; key partner departures could cut revenue. Valuation of 22x to 24x is not cheap for a cyclical business.

## What would prove the idea wrong
Another quarter of revenue declines above 10% with pretax margin below about 17%; compensation ratio above about 70%; senior partner departures; restructuring revenue fading without strategic advisory replacing it; cash and investments falling below about 500M with buybacks continuing; announced deal volumes dropping sharply.
