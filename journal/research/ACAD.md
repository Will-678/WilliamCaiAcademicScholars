## Countercase
Cash flow is small relative to the price: TTM FCF 82M is 2.5% of market cap and SBC consumes most of it; SG&A grew 25% in 9M26 (490.5M vs 393.3M) and 9M R&D is 234.7M. About 56% of 9M revenue is Nuplazid, whose generic-entry timing, patents and settlements were not read, and Daybue growth depends on a rare-disease base and a pricing and payer environment not yet checked. The Alzheimer's psychosis program (remlifanserin) missed its Phase 2 primary endpoint and Phase 3 spending continues. The head of R&D retired (8-K 2026-10-23, consultant to 2026-12-31). Low reported P/E is a tax artifact. 9M26 working-capital, rebate accruals and gross-to-net trends unread. A derivative suit settlement (preliminary approval 2026-08-24) was disclosed, terms unread.

## What would change the view
Thesis breaks if Nuplazid sales turn negative or generic entry is earlier than assumed, Daybue growth falls below about 15% with margin pressure, SG&A growth keeps outrunning revenue so operating income stalls, Phase 3 ADP studies fail after continued spending, or net cash is used on a dilutive acquisition.
