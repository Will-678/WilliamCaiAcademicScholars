## Counterthesis
Cash is not owned free and clear: about 109M of annualized operating spend means roughly 2.5 years of runway, and the ATM (about 100M available) signals dilution risk if the stock rises or trials need funding. Takeda can terminate for convenience or on safety, leaving no further milestones; open-label Phase 2 elritercept data may overstate effects; the price may be low because the market expects Phase 3 failure, a cheaper or competing MDS anemia therapy, or because the earlier cibotercept program was stopped. Insider Form 144 filings on 11-12 and 11-17 and a Form 4 on 11-16 were seen in the filing list but not read. The 11-17 rise of 12.8% was not explained. Screen figures (2025 margin, score 97.8) are windfall driven and must not be read as quality.

## Invalidation
The net cash discount thesis fails if Takeda terminates or pauses the elritercept Phase 3 program, if safety signals appear, if cash falls well below 200M without milestones, if the company issues stock below cash value through the ATM, or if the price rises above cash plus a modest pipeline value so no discount remains.
