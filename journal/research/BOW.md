## Counterthesis

Going-private deals with an existing 14.3% holder carry conflict risk; eleven demand letters and two complaints seek to enjoin it. CFO Mulcahey announced departure in April 2027. Valuation at 2.5x book and 18.8x TTM NI reflects the buyer's premium, not an organic discount. Reserve adequacy for casualty lines (reserves 1,432M against equity 451M) was not examined.
