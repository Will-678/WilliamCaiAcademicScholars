## Counterthesis
Earnings are helped by a tax rate down 7 points and a warranty release; at the old rate TTM earnings would be lower, so the multiple is higher than 23x. FCF yield 2.6% (1.8% after SBC) is low because capex is large (249M TTM, 113.9M in 9M26 versus 56.5M a year earlier). Debt 962M plus a 1B buyback authorization raises leverage if funded with debt. A cannula tear correction signals product quality risk; a securities class action (Hu v. Insulet, filed 2026-07-02) and the tariff exemption for medical devices are open risks. Competition in automated insulin delivery, GLP-1 effects on diabetes demand, and reimbursement were not reviewed (MD&A truncated). US growth slowed to 14.7% versus 31.7% international.
