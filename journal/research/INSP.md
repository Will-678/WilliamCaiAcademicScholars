The market may be right that the competition or coding problem is structural; the new CPT code does not start until 2028. Adjusted EPS excludes SBC, which is about 13% of TTM revenue. The 10-Q MD&A, risk factors, competitor (e.g. Nyxoah, LivaNova) and GLP-1 demand effects, inventory and receivables were not read; the 13G/A flurry 11-09 to 11-16 was not read (could be holder sales).
