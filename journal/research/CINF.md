Nine month combined ratio of 95.8% is inside the 92 to 98 target but leaves little margin and 2026 benefited from 2.9 points of favorable reserve development, which may fade. Growth is slowing (NWP +2% in Q3) as commercial pricing softens. Cat exposure (severe convective storms) and equity market sensitivity are the thesis breakers.
