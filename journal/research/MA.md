## Countercase
A 34.6x trailing multiple and 2.8% FCF yield leave little margin: a deceleration to high single digits or an interchange or surcharge regulatory change would compress it. Regulation (U.S. merchant litigation, UK and EU interchange cases, Credit Card Competition Act style proposals), stablecoin and account-to-account disruption, tariffs on cross-border travel and debt-funded buybacks (equity only 6.8B) are risks. Litigation accrual dropped to 262M, so earlier charges may reverse the tailwind. It is also a large holding in broad US index funds, so exposure duplicates the core allocation.

## What would change the view
Thesis breaks if net revenue growth falls below about 10% for several quarters, cross-border volume turns negative, a binding interchange cap is enacted, or a large unaccrued settlement hits.
