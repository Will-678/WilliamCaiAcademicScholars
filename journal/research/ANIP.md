The Egetis deal adds about 560M of debt-funded acquisition, a short bridge needing refinancing in capital markets, secured liens on substantially all assets, and a rare-disease asset (not yet characterized from filings). Acquired-intangible and product-right payments (5.3M in 9M) are small but historically the company grows by acquisition. SBC is high at 3-4% of revenue. 22.7M shares means each large block or secondary moves price.
