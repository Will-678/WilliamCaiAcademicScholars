Standalone, leverage is 4.2x with a 5.0x covenant at Aireon and 183.4M seller debt due about July 2027; OEBITDA fell in Q3; GAAP earnings are inflated by the gain. Government revenue is about 29% of 9M sales and the EMSS contract extension runs only to March 2027.
