## Counterthesis
Operating income fell 8% in Q3 and 5% in 9M despite 6% TPV growth; transaction expense grows faster than revenue; restructuring charges 255M in Q3 (442M 9M) are recurring across 2025 and 2026 plans. OCF in 9M rose 17% but Q3 OCF fell 19% (1,592M vs 1,974M), and cash flow includes loan receivable and customer fund timing, so FCF yield of 13% may overstate owner earnings. 1.5B of Japan installment loans were moved to held for sale and consumer loans fell 19%, so credit exposure is changing. Active accounts grow 1%. Multiple legal matters: branded checkout securities class action, CFPB civil investigative demands on Regulation E and PayPal Credit, an EU proceeding, derivative suits (outcomes unread). Debt rose (long term 13.4B at 9/30 per XBRL tag vs 10.9B at March) while cash fell. Peers (Block, Adyen, Fiserv, Visa) and own multiple history not compared; MD&A margin drivers read only in part.
