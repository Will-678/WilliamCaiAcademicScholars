## Countercase
Growth is price-led: ad price +34% and ARPU +36% against DAUq +12%, with US DAUq shrinking, and the company says search and AI-summary traffic changes may continue or worsen; an advertiser pullback or a Google ranking change would hit a business with 91.5% gross margin and heavy fixed costs. Revenue growth guided from 53% to about 40% in Q4. GAAP profit is flattered: income tax was only 11.5M on 751.6M pre-tax income (1.5%) because of a full US valuation allowance, so tax-normalized earnings at 21% are about 790M and the P/E about 24x (own approximation); the allowance release will create a one-time gain that must not be treated as earnings. Stock compensation is large (300.6M in 9M; SBC and related taxes 322.1M excluded from adjusted EBITDA), so FCF yield after SBC is 4.3% rather than 6.3%. Buybacks (340.0M in 9M, 660.0M remaining) offset part of the dilution; net share count rose in 2026 anyway (Class A plus B 190.9M at 12/31/2025 to 193.1M at 9/30/2026). Data licensing (other revenue 48.0M in Q3, 130.0M in 9M, 5.5% of revenue) is small and depends on AI customers; scraping and AI-answer substitutes are named risks. Cloud commitments of 880M (AWS) and 580M (Google) run to 2029. Advance Magazine Publishers holds about 22% and the dual class structure leaves public holders limited influence. Competition from Google, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Snap, and AI assistants.

## Invalidation
US DAUq declining by more than about 5% y/y or logged-in users turning negative; ad price growth falling below about 15% while impressions do not offset; revenue growth guided below about 25%; further search-referral losses; a data-licensing contract not renewed; operating margin falling below about 20% (Q3 29%); buybacks ceasing while SBC rises.
