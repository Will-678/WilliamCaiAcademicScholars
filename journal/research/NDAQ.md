## Countercase
At 29x GAAP earnings and a price within 3% of the 52-week high, the market already pays for double-digit net revenue growth. Market Services growth (+26% in Q3) depends on elevated trading volumes and may reverse. Net debt of about 8.2B from the Adenza deal limits flexibility, tax rate rose to 22.9% from 18.9%, 9M includes an 84M divestiture gain, and buybacks accelerated to 1.2B (pace funded partly by commercial paper). TTM revenue combines bases that were restated (9M25 6,138M originally vs 6,175M now). Segment net revenue, ARR, non-GAAP reconciliation, guidance, litigation, Adenza integration and peers (ICE, CME, CBOE, LSEG, TW) unread.

## What would change the view
Thesis weakens if net revenue growth drops below high single digits when volumes normalize, Financial Technology ARR growth slows, or leverage rises. A valuation case needs a price near a 5% FCF yield or evidence of durable low-teens growth.
