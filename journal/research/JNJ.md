The talc charge is large and unquantified in what I read; a proposed resolution is not final and cash payments will reduce FCF over time. GAAP earnings are dominated by special items; TTM GAAP 42.8x is a poor valuation basis and adjusted EPS excludes recurring acquisition and litigation costs. Adjusted EPS fell 7.5% in Q3 and guidance implies only +2.9% for 2026, while the stock is at 25.7x adjusted, near its 52-week high. MedTech growth is only +2.5% and international +1.5%. Stelara biosimilar erosion, tariffs, drug price negotiation and Medicare pricing, and the Intra-Cellular acquisition integration are unreviewed. Peers (LLY, ABBV, MRK, PFE, ABT) not compared; JNJ is a large holding in broad US index funds, so it duplicates index exposure.
