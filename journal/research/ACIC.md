## Counterthesis
Earnings are declining and premiums are shrinking about 12% a year; 5.4x earnings is the market pricing a normal hurricane year or reserve or reinsurance cost normalization, not an anomaly. A single Florida hurricane could erase several years of earnings; the 2026-27 reinsurance program, retention and prior-year reserve development were not read. Debt of 149.6M against equity 355.7M, a retained deficit, and 59.5M goodwill reduce tangible equity (about 296M, so about 1.6x tangible book). The reserve drop from 165.7M to 95.2M needs explanation (run-off, commutation or sale). Peers (HCI, HRTG, UVE, KINS) not compared; UVE is already a watch at 5.8x and similar peak-cycle logic.

## Invalidation
The low multiple view fails if a major Florida hurricane or adverse reserve development hits, if reinsurance renewal costs jump, if premiums keep falling faster than expenses, or if quarterly earnings keep dropping so TTM NI falls below about 60M.
