## Countercase
The low multiple may be fair: total revenue is shrinking, GAAP operating margin fell from 30.2% to 27.2% (9M), hardware refresh product revenue fell 20% in Q3 and management only expects gradual improvement beginning in Q4 with no numeric guidance. SBC is high (about 8% of revenue). A 2B convertible with capped calls was issued while buying back about 975M of stock: this is leverage-assisted EPS support, and conversion terms (price, maturity, coupon) are unread. The 31% drop from the 52-week high is not evidence of cheapness. Israel geopolitical risk, security-platform competition (Palo Alto, Fortinet, Zscaler) and cash held partly in long-term marketable securities (1,969M) also matter. Peers, 20-F and convertible indenture unread.

## What would change the view
Thesis weakens if subscriptions growth falls below high single digits, total revenue declines again in Q4 and Q1, or operating margin falls below 25%. Valuation case needs sustained FCF yield above 6% after SBC with revenue stabilizing.
