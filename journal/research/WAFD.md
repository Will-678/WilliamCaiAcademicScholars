The FY2026 10-K is due about now and is not yet filed.

## Counterthesis
A low multiple may reflect that WaFd holders become a 40.8% minority in a larger, differently risked bank; EverBank is not public, so its earnings quality, deposit mix and credit were not checked. Deposits shrank 2.4% in nine months while liabilities rose. The 2.85% fall on 11-17 and 4% decline since 11-13 are unexplained. Stock issuance approval is a vote on 2026-12-16 and could fail. Merger accretion claims (29% EPS accretion for 2027) are management projections.
