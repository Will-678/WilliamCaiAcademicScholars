Equity outflows of 61.7B in nine months, rising to 25.6B in Q3 alone, and fee rate decline (39.1 to 37.8 bps) mean revenue growth depends on market levels; a 20% market drop would cut revenue quickly with 38 to 40% margins on a mostly fixed cost base. Q3 net income and EPS fell year over year; adjusted EPS grew only 1.8%. Adjusted expenses rose 5.8% while headcount fell, with higher bonus accrual. Cash flow includes consolidated investment product effects and seed investments; TTM OCF is a reported figure not adjusted for those. Dividend plus buybacks absorb 81% of FCF; 4.8% yield is attractive but only if flows stabilize. Passive and ETF share gains, fee compression and competitor moves are structural risks, and the Q3 loss of the multi-asset category (-7.6B) shows outflows are broadening.
