Valuation is the problem: 124x GAAP and 24x sales with a 1.5% FCF yield. The gap between adjusted EPS (about 9.6) and GAAP (about 2.5 TTM) comes from SBC (56.7M TTM, 42.4M in 9M26), intangible amortization, deal costs and fair value marks that recur for an acquisitive royalty buyer. Leverage rose from 0.45B to 1.16B principal; cash fell to 447M from 734M. The stock is only about 5% below its 52-week high and analysts have been raising targets. Revenue concentration in a few royalties (Filspari, Ohtuvayre, Vabysmo) and patent or competitive loss, integration cost overruns and a failed capital allocation would break the thesis.
