## Countercase
INGREZZA is still the profit engine (about 75% of 9M product sales) and faces a Zydus Paragraph IV challenge on INGREZZA SPRINKLE, Teva's AUSTEDO XR, a DOJ civil investigative demand on its sales and marketing (August 2025, no outcome) and the Inflation Reduction Act (applicability to INGREZZA not checked in the 10-Q read). VYKAT XR is already challenged by an Alvogen ANDA (September 2026; the suit triggered a 30-month stay, exclusivities run to a date not read). The acquisition added 2,191.0M of intangibles and 500.6M of goodwill (preliminary allocation) and a pending Soleno securities class action; Q3 included a 52.3M unrealized loss on equity investments; receivables rose 270.5M (957.3M from 686.8M); noncurrent inventory of 177.7M mainly carries acquisition step-up. TTM FCF benefits from strong collections of a fast-growing receivable and a 103.6M income tax asset swing, and Q3 revenue growth may not repeat as CRENESSITY and VYKAT XR comparisons toughen. Stock fell 3.8% on 2026-11-05 then recovered to 168.86.

## Invalidation
INGREZZA volume turning negative or net price erosion beyond about 5%, CRENESSITY quarterly sales stalling below about 200M, an adverse Zydus or Alvogen ruling, DOJ action with a monetary penalty, or VYKAT XR sales undershooting the acquisition case.
