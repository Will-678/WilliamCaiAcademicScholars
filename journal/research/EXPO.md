## Countercase
At about 37x TTM earnings and 3% FCF yield after SBC the market pays a premium for steady mid-teens EBITDA growth; guidance implies Q4 growth slows to 8-10% and margin falls about 3 points year on year in guidance terms. Management repurchased shares at about 65 and now they trade 33% higher; payouts exceeded cash generation and cash fell by half, so buyback capacity is limited unless it borrows. Total revenue growth of 18% includes pass-through reimbursements; the underlying figure is 12%. Litigation and dispute demand is cyclical, utilization of 77% leaves little further margin gain, and 22M of SBC is a real cost. Peers, MD&A, segment margins, backlog-like indicators, concentration and the 10-K were not read.

## Invalidation
