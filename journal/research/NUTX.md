## Countercase
Revenue per visit fell by roughly a third year on year while volumes grew, which suggests prior revenue estimates were too high and receivables of 356M (163 days) may carry further write-downs; operating cash flow 258M TTM is not clearly separate from collections of old balances. Earnings are inflated by a 52.3M non-cash contract services credit from the HaloMD amendment, and adjusted EBITDA includes it. The business depends on No Surprises Act arbitration outcomes, third-party arbitration provider HaloMD, and insurer lawsuits against that provider; a rule change or adverse rulings would hit revenue directly. Finance lease liabilities of 295M (mostly real estate) are debt like and larger than reported debt of 43M. Noncontrolling interests take about 28% of consolidated net income. Share count is tiny (6.9M) and daily volume 80k to 200k shares, so price moves are large (-15% in a week, 171 to 189 in a day) and liquidity is thin. Cyber breach exposes patient data with litigation and regulatory cost unquantified. Securities class actions and earn-out disputes are in the risk list. MD&A, legal proceedings and 10-K were not read, peers (HCA, UHS, other emergency operators) not compared. Share count history (about 6.9M shares, likely a reverse split) not verified.
