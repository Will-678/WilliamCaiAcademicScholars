Adjusted net income (9M 208.2M) exceeds GAAP (160.9M) by about 47M of excluded items, a gap the 10-Q reconciliation shows but this record did not decompose. Q3 had 4.0M of adverse prior year development (crop reallocation) against 20.6M favorable over nine months; catastrophe losses in Hawaii and Midwest floods; total liabilities rose from 2,108.3M to 3,184.3M in nine months, assets 3,051.0M to 4,169.2M, so leverage (liabilities to assets 76%) and reinsurance reliance rose; interest expense went from 0.3M to 13.2M for nine months on the term loan and an FHLB line. Other underwriting expenses +59.8%. At 4.2x book the stock depends on sustaining a very high ROE. The stock rose 8.8% in a week (144.04 on 11-04 to 156.78 on 11-09), so part of any discount to its recent range has closed.

## Invalidation
A combined ratio persistently above about 85%, material adverse reserve development, a large catastrophe loss exceeding reinsurance or reinsurance renewal on sharply worse terms, growth in crop or surety lines turning into underwriting losses, or ROE falling below about 15%.
