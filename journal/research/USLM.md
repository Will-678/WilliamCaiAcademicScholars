## What the price seems to assume
Continued high single digit growth and margin recovery once the Texas kiln runs well. The latest nine months show flat profit, so the evidence for acceleration is not yet in the filings.

## Countercase
Costs (diesel, transport, kiln integration) are eroding gross profit while sales growth slowed to 3%; construction and shingle demand fell; the multiple is not low. High quality, but quality is priced.

## Invalidation and what would change the view
Gross profit still falling in Q4 and Q1 after kiln integration would invalidate. A pullback toward about 20x TTM earnings with stable margins, or visible Texas volume and margin gains, would upgrade it.
